## 4. Future Expansion Roadmap: Scaling with Account Growth

As the account compounds into 6-figure and 7-figure scale ($\$100,000 \to \$500,000+$), the portfolio dynamics shift dramatically:

### 4.1 Sizing Dynamics at Scale
* Under Andy's banded risk framework:
  * Accounts below $\$10\text{k}$: $10\%$ balance risk ($70$ to $\$1,000$/trade).
  * Accounts at $\$10\text{k} \to \$100\text{k}$: $\$1,000$ per full $\$10\text{k}$ step.
  * Accounts at $\$100\text{k}+$: Capped at **$\$1,000$ to $\$10,000$ fixed risk per trade**.
* **Percentage Exposure at Scale:**
  * When an account reaches $\$500,000$, a $\$10,000$ trade represents only **$2.0\%$ of total account equity**.
  * A $\$2,500$ trade represents only **$0.5\%$ of account equity**.

### 4.2 Lifting the Position Ceiling to 5 Positions
On a $\$250\text{k} \to \$500\text{k}+$ account, the portfolio rules can safely expand from a 3-position cap to a **5-position cap**:
* **Total Exposure Math:**
  $$\text{Max Risk} = 5 \text{ positions} \times 2.0\% \text{ risk/trade} = 10.0\% \text{ total account risk}$$
* **Safety Margin:** $10.0\%$ total risk sits comfortably below the **$20\%$ maximum portfolio risk ceiling**, leaving **$90\%$ of account equity untouched** and easily clearing the **$30\%$ free margin reserve floor**.
* **BTC Sovereign Priority Maintained:** Even if all 4 alts (SOL, ETH, ADA, LINK) are in open trades simultaneously, Bitcoin Traveler retains an open slot and ample margin to execute without compromise.

### 4.3 Coincidence Reality (Extreme Rarity of Simultaneous Fills)
Historical audit across 4.5 years proves that all 5 assets (BTC + 4 alts) holding open positions simultaneously is an **extreme rarity**:
* Overlap of 3 positions: $2.3\%$ of trading days.

## 5. Formal Alt Matrix Qualification Criteria

To qualify any future altcoin for admission into the Alt Matrix, the asset must satisfy the following 6 quantitative screening standards:

### Criterion 1: Institutional Tenure & Cycle History
* **Requirement:** Must have at least **3 to 5 years of continuous, verified trading history** on major exchanges.
* **Rationale:** Eliminates new speculative tokens that have only existed in a bull market. The asset must have proven structural survival through a major crypto winter (bear market drawdown $\ge 75\%$).

### Criterion 2: Liquidity & Order Book Depth
* **Requirement:** Top 10 to Top 20 market cap with deep open interest and tight bid-ask spreads ($< 2\text{ bps}$) on Bitunix perpetual futures.
* **Rationale:** When trading at scale ($\$1,000 \to \$10,000$ risk units, requiring $\$50\text{k} \to \$250\text{k}$ notional contract size), execution cannot suffer from slippage or thin order books on market entry and stop execution.

### Criterion 3: Macro Regime Cleanliness (Daily 200 SMA)
* **Requirement:** Clean structural adherence to the 200-day Simple Moving Average.
* **Test:** The asset must trade decisively above the 200 SMA during macro expansions, and remain suppressed below it during bear markets. Assets that repeatedly oscillate/chop across the 200 SMA on daily closes fail qualification.

### Criterion 4: 4H Trend Persistence (Silver Cross)
* **Requirement:** Positive net expectancy ($> +0.40\text{R/trade}$) on 4H 21/55 EMA crossovers.
* **Test:** When a Silver Cross occurs above the Daily 200 SMA, the crossover must resolve into a sustained directional impulse rather than immediate mean reversion.

### Criterion 5: Fat-Tail Runner Payoff Asymmetry
* **Requirement:** Maximum historical runner payoff must exceed **$+12.0\text{R}$**, with win-loss payoff ratio $\ge 2.5:1$.
* **Rationale:** The Alt Matrix edge is not built on a 70% win rate (win rate is $35\% \to 47\%$). The entire mathematical edge comes from holding multi-week runners ($+5\text{R}, +10\text{R}, +20\text{R}$) while cutting losers at $-1.0\text{R}$. Assets with muted volatility or capped upside (like LTC) bleed on stop-outs.

### Criterion 6: Funding Rate Stability
* **Requirement:** 8-hour perpetual funding rate must stay within normal bounds ($< +0.05\%$) during standard market conditions.
* **Rationale:** Heavily crowded retail meme tokens often trade at chronic extreme positive funding, causing the system to constantly veto entries or suffer severe carry drag.
